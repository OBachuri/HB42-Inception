PacMan

Dive into a fast-paced arcade maze chase inspired by classic Pac-Man gameplay.

Collect pacgums, power up with super pellets, and outmaneuver four ghosts across increasingly challenging levels.

Each run tests your reflexes, routing, and risk-taking as you race against level timers and hunt for a top score.
Enter your name at the end to claim a spot in the persistent Top 10 highscores.
Easy to start and hard to master, it’s a retro-style challenge built for quick sessions and replayability.
